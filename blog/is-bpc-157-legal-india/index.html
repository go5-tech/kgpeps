Is BPC-157 Legal in India?

Published 2026-07-05 · Peptide Central Research Team

BPC-157 is one of the most searched research peptides in India, and the most common question buyers ask is whether it is legal to purchase and possess. The short answer: BPC-157 is legal to buy in India for laboratory and analytical research use. It is not a scheduled or narcotic substance, but it is also not an approved medicine for human use — and that distinction is what matters.

Is BPC-157 a controlled substance in India?

No. BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic peptide fragment and is not listed as a narcotic or psychotropic substance under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985. It is not a controlled substance, so buying, selling and possessing it for research does not fall foul of India’s drug-control laws in the way controlled drugs do.

Is BPC-157 approved for human use in India?

No. BPC-157 has not received marketing approval from the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) as a drug for human treatment. Under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, a compound cannot be legally marketed for human consumption without such approval. This is why every legitimate Indian supplier — including Peptide Central — labels BPC-157 strictly “for research use only.” Selling it with human dosing instructions or health claims is what crosses the legal line.

The research-use framework

Compounds intended for in-vitro laboratory and analytical research — not administered to humans or animals — generally sit outside consumer drug regulation. BPC-157 is supplied on this basis. Responsible buyers should understand that the legality rests on the research-use context: the compound is legal to obtain for research, and the responsibility for how it is used rests with the buyer.
