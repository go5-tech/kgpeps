Is Tirzepatide Legal in India?

Published 2026-07-05 · Peptide Central Research Team

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist and one of the most searched weight-management research peptides in India. The common question is whether it is legal to buy. The short answer: Tirzepatide is legal to purchase in India for laboratory research use, as a research compound rather than a dispensed medicine.

Is Tirzepatide a controlled substance in India?

No. Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide and is not listed under the NDPS Act, 1985. It is not a narcotic, psychotropic or otherwise controlled substance, so buying and possessing it for research is not restricted in that category.

Is Tirzepatide approved for human use in India?

Approved GLP-1/GIP medicines are prescription products dispensed through pharmacies. Research-grade Tirzepatide sold by peptide suppliers is a different legal category — an unapproved research compound, labelled strictly “for research use only.” It cannot legally be marketed for human consumption without CDSCO approval, so any supplier attaching human dosing instructions or treatment claims is operating outside the law.

The research-use framework

As with other research peptides, Tirzepatide is supplied on the basis that it is for in-vitro laboratory and analytical use and is not administered to humans. This keeps it outside consumer drug regulation, and it places responsibility for use on the buyer. For a compound comparison, see Retatrutide vs Tirzepatide.
