Peptide Pricing FAQ

How much do peptides cost in India?

Research peptide prices in India range from around ₹2,999 for entry compounds like MOTS-C, Semax and Ipamorelin up to ₹10,999 for larger vials of Tirzepatide. Every price on this page is the current live price at Peptide Central, in INR, inclusive of a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis.

Why do peptide prices vary so much?

Price depends on the peptide's synthesis complexity, vial size (mg), and purity grade. Longer or harder-to-synthesise sequences and larger vials cost more. Suspiciously cheap peptides are usually underdosed or unverified — always check for an HPLC Certificate of Analysis.

Is Cash on Delivery available for peptides in India?

Yes. Peptide Central offers Cash on Delivery (COD) pan-India, alongside UPI and IMPS. Delivery is free and typically takes 3–5 business days with tracking.

Are these peptide prices GST-inclusive?

Listed prices are the final amount you pay in INR. Shipping is free across India, so the price shown is the price delivered.
